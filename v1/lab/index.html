Three instruments. They show how a leak is looked at. They are not a forecast, and they are not a client result. Motion follows the pause control in the header.

Funnel model

Sample, not a client: 1,000 visits, 4% leave a request, 20% of those become customers. That is 40 leads and 8 customers. The larger share is lost between the visit and the lead. Edit the fields to move the model.

With the sample figures: 40 leads, 8 customers. The page and the offer are the first place to look.

Model assumptions

Leads equal visits times the visit-to-lead rate. Customers equal leads times the lead-to-customer rate. The larger loss is the step that drops a greater share of the people who reached it. Spend, if you enter it, is divided by those counts. Nothing here predicts your revenue.
