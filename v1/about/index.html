About the studio

swg.studio is a separate site for the web design and digital marketing work of Support Wellness Global. It is a working digital systems lab. The interaction on this site is here to show a business system, not to decorate a homepage.

What wellness means

Healthier businesses. Clear offers, owned handoffs, and measurements a team can use. Not spa branding, and not a yoga practice. If a page here sounds clinical, that is a mistake. The subject is the operating health of a company.

Who directs it

Will Wiedenmann directs SWG Studio. The parent firm is Support Wellness Global. Company-wide operating systems, and work that needs a different scope than the three studio plans, stay with the parent firm.

This page does not invent a client list, a headcount, or a set of results. Proof will be published when a client has approved it. Until then, the work index holds templates and says so.

How the studio behaves

Diagnose, align, rebuild. Strong parts, strong connections. The pause control in the header stops motion on the page. If your system asks for reduced motion, the living map steps down to a list and the control reads "Motion off."

The parent site at supportwellnessglobal.com uses the same idea on its coherence example: motion pauses when you read, stops off screen, and yields to reduced motion. This studio keeps that discipline and points it at the digital system itself.
