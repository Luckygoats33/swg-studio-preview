The reading

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Conversion & Analytics

If you cannot read the handoff, you cannot tell which fix worked.

A definition of a conversion, the events that match it, and a reading the operator will actually open.

  • We cannot measure conversions
  • Reports describe traffic and not customers
  • Spend changes without a shared number

Traffic is not a conversion. A pageview is not a lead. A lead is not a customer. Teams get stuck because the tools are happy to chart the first of those and quiet about the last. SWG installs a reading that matches the decision you are trying to make.

The break

The account has tags. The tags do not agree with the business. One pixel fires on every button. Another fires nowhere. The CRM has a stage called “new” that includes people who never heard back. The weekly meeting looks at sessions because sessions are the only number everyone trusts, and it is the wrong number.

Paid media then “optimizes” toward that confusion. The leak gets a budget.

The work

The work usually includes:

  • A written definition of lead, qualified conversation, and customer
  • An event map from the page and the ads into the record
  • Implementation of those events, including what is deliberately not tracked
  • A consent approach you have approved with counsel. We do not give legal advice.
  • A short reading: the few numbers that change a decision, and the person who looks at them
  • A note on what would falsify the current plan

Foundation includes the basic events on the site. Growth System and Interactive Flagship make the reading part of how spend and follow-up are run.

What we need from you

The decision the reading is for. Access to analytics, ads, and the CRM. Someone who knows which inquiries were actually good. Patience for a dull week of definition before any new dashboard.

Connections

Analytics depends on the website to fire a meaningful event and on the CRM to confirm that a human accepted the handoff. Ads depend on analytics before the budget moves. A beautiful site with no reading can still leak. You just will not see where.

What good looks like

The team can say, in one sentence, what counted as a conversion last week. That sentence matches the CRM. The ad account is aimed at it, or is being held until it can be. Arguments about performance use the same denominator.

No benchmark from another company is presented as your target. Your baseline is measured, then improved, then shown to you for approval before it is called a result.

Where this sits

  • Foundation, $12k-$20k. A website that finally says what the business has become.
  • Growth System, $25k-$45k. Not just a better site. A connected acquisition system.
  • Interactive Flagship, $45k-$85k+. Build the site people remember, and the system your team can actually run.
