Plans

Three starting ranges for the studio. Each one names a boundary. If the break is outside the boundary, we say so before the work starts.

Foundation

$12k-$20k

A website that finally says what the business has become.

  1. Message and site architecture
  2. Design and build of the marketing site
  3. One clear next step on the page
  4. Basic conversion events
  5. Accessibility and performance pass
Timeline
Often 6-10 weeks once scope is agreed. Planning range, not a promise.
Best for
The offer is real and the site is behind the business. You need the public instrument corrected before you add channels.

Growth System

$25k-$45k

Not just a better site. A connected acquisition system.

  1. The foundation site, plus the path from attention to a record
  2. Offer alignment between ads and the page
  3. A handoff with an owner, into a CRM or a shared inbox
  4. Measurement the team can read on a set rhythm
  5. A short operating note for the people who run it
Timeline
Often 10-16 weeks once scope is agreed. Planning range, not a promise.
Best for
Demand exists, and it leaks between the ad, the site, and the follow-up.

Interactive Flagship

$45k-$85k+

Build the site people remember, and the system your team can actually run.

  1. Interaction that explains the business, not a decorative effect
  2. The acquisition path and the measurement from Growth System
  3. A design and content system the team can extend
  4. Handoffs written down and built
  5. A lab surface for experiments after launch
Timeline
Often 16-24 weeks once scope is agreed. Planning range, not a promise.
Best for
You need a public presence people remember and an internal system the team can run without the founder in the middle.

Growth & Optimization $3k-$8k/month

Keep the system in register after the build: experiments, measurement, and fixes as the offer changes.

Optional. Not a substitute for the build. Teams that have a working path and need it tended. Monthly, after a build or a defined review.

The number is not the mystery. The scope is.

Ranges are starting points. Scope, timing, and fee are set in the agreement before work begins. Larger operating-system builds stay with Support Wellness Global and are scoped separately.

How to read a range

A range is not a quote. Scope, timing, and fee are written down before work begins. The timelines above are planning ranges for a conversation. They are not a delivery promise.

Foundation is the public instrument. Growth System connects that instrument to acquisition and a record. Interactive Flagship is for a company that needs the public site and the operating path built so the team can run both.

Broader operating-system work, company ERP, and custom product builds stay with Support Wellness Global and are scoped separately.
