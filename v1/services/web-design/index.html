The public instrument

  1. Home

A website that finally says what the business has become.

Message, structure, design, and build for the public instrument of the offer. One next step. The handoff is named before launch.

  • The site looks dated
  • Nobody can repeat the offer
  • The page has several competing next steps

The website is the part of the system a buyer can see. If it still describes a former offer, every ad and every sales call has to correct it. That correction is expensive, and it fails in silence. The people who leave do not write to explain why.

The break

A dated site is not only a visual problem. It is usually a message problem that has been covered with a theme. The company changed. The page did not. Or the page says five true things and asks for three actions, so the buyer takes none of them.

Traffic with no leads often lands here as well. The ad was specific. The page is general. The form asks for a conversation before it says what the conversation is for.

The work

SWG writes the offer in language a buyer can repeat, then builds the site around that sentence.

The work usually includes:

  • A message hierarchy: who it is for, what it does, and what happens next
  • Page structure that follows the decision, not a template’s default sections
  • Design that can be maintained: type, color, components, and rules for using them
  • A build that is fast, accessible, and editable by the people who own the words
  • One primary next step, with the owner of that step named before launch
  • The events a later measurement pass will need

Foundation is this work on its own. Growth System and Interactive Flagship include it, then connect it to ads, the record, and the reading.

What we need from you

Someone who can say what the business will not do, and what it actually sells now. Access to the current site, recent inquiries, and the people who answer them. You do not need a finished brand book. You do need a decision-maker in the room.

Connections

The site depends on content for the claim and on the CRM for the handoff. Ads depend on the site to keep the promise the click made. Analytics depends on the site to fire an event that means something.

A redesign that does not name the handoff gives you a calmer page and the same leak.

What good looks like

A new visitor can say what you do, who it is for, and what to do next without a call. The team can change the words without breaking the layout. The page can be read with a keyboard, with reduced motion, and on a phone. The next step creates a record someone owns.

We do not publish invented conversion rates. A real engagement reports before and after only when you approve the numbers.

Where this sits

  • Foundation, $12k-$20k. A website that finally says what the business has become.
  • Growth System, $25k-$45k. Not just a better site. A connected acquisition system.
  • Interactive Flagship, $45k-$85k+. Build the site people remember, and the system your team can actually run.

The rest of the system

  • Conversion & Analytics. The reading.
