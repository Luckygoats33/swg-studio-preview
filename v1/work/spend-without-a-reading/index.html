Spend without a reading

A template for documenting paid media that cannot be judged. This is not a client engagement and it reports no results.

  • Automated
  • Launched
  • Experimented
Before, intervention, after

Live spend, no definition

Ads run against a page that does not share a definition of success with the people who answer the phone. Reports describe traffic.

Define the conversion, then connect it

The team writes what counts as a lead. The page, the record, and the ad account are pointed at that definition. Experiments wait until the reading exists.

A channel that can be judged

The template ends when the path can be read. It does not end with a winner. Test results are not invented here.

This is the second template. Use it when the work is a launch or an experiment that was flying without a reading. Do not use it to imply that a channel “scaled.” Scaled is a filter on the work index, and it stays empty until a client-approved study belongs there.

The intervention in this template is deliberately boring. Define the conversion. Match the ad to the page. Give the lead a record. Then, and only then, run the test. A test without that sequence produces a chart, not a decision.

Story mode carries that sequence. Proof mode is where deliverables, stack, accessibility, performance, and the empty outcome slot live. Both are in the page without a script. The script only records that a reader opened Proof mode.

Objective
Make the spend answer to a definition of a lead the team agrees on.
Problem
Campaigns are live. The page can collect a form. Nothing reliable says which click became a conversation, or which conversation became a customer. Budget moves on opinion.
Intervention
Align the ad and the page, define the conversion, record the handoff, and only then treat the channel as something that can be tested.
Deliverables
  • Offer match between ad and page
  • Conversion definition
  • Event map from click to record
  • Routing rule so the lead is not an email forward
  • A weekly reading the operator can open
Stack

Illustrative for this template, not a client's production list.

  • Ad account
  • Landing page
  • Tag or event stream
  • CRM or shared record
Interaction notes
Proof mode is the audit surface. Story mode is the sequence. The scrubber is the explanation a buyer can operate without a deck. None of it is a claim about a real account.
Accessibility
No audit score is published. Measurement UI in a real engagement must be readable without color alone, and pauseable if any status animates.
Performance
No performance score is published. Landing pages in a real engagement are held to the same Core Web Vitals bar as the rest of the studio site, and the numbers are reported only after they are measured.
Outcomes
No cost per lead, return, or lift is stated. Those fields stay blank until a client approves them.
Testimonial
None. Do not add a quote until the client has approved the words and the attribution.
