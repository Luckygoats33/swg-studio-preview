Services

Four practices. One path from a public claim to a record someone owns, and a reading the team will open. Hiring one of them while the connection is broken usually recreates the leak.

  1. 01 The public instrumentWeb Design & DevelopmentA website that finally says what the business has become.
  2. 02 The path from spend to the pageGrowth MarketingPaid attention is a leak until the path can hold it.
  3. 03 The handoff, with an ownerAI & AutomationIf the team is the integration, the system is not finished.
  4. 04 The readingConversion & AnalyticsIf you cannot read the handoff, you cannot tell which fix worked.

Web Design & Development

Message, structure, design, and build for the public instrument of the offer. One next step. The handoff is named before launch.

  • The site looks dated
  • Nobody can repeat the offer
  • The page has several competing next steps

Growth Marketing

Offer, ads, and landing path treated as one system. Spend is not raised until the page and the reading can hold it.

  • Paid media is leaking money
  • The ad and the page make different promises
  • Spend moves without a reading

AI & Automation

Routing, ownership, and follow-up so the founder is not the middleware. Automation removes copying. It does not hide the handoff.

  • Handoffs live in an inbox
  • People retype the same lead into another tool
  • Follow-up depends on memory

Conversion & Analytics

A definition of a conversion, the events that match it, and a reading the operator will actually open.

  • We cannot measure conversions
  • Reports describe traffic and not customers
  • Spend changes without a shared number
