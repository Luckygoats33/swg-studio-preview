The path from spend to the page

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Growth Marketing

Paid attention is a leak until the path can hold it.

Offer, ads, and landing path treated as one system. Spend is not raised until the page and the reading can hold it.

  • Paid media is leaking money
  • The ad and the page make different promises
  • Spend moves without a reading

Buying more attention is the expensive way to learn that the path leaks. SWG treats the ad, the page, and the first reply as one system. If those three disagree, the account is not a growth engine. It is a bill.

The break

The usual report says the campaign is “working” because the click price looks acceptable. Further down the path, the page hedges, the form is vague, and the reply is slow. The cost that matters is the cost of a real conversation, and often nobody has defined one.

A second break is the claim itself. The ad is sharp because it had to be. The website still speaks in categories. People feel the gap and leave.

The work

Growth marketing here is not a stack of new channels. It is the repair of the path you already pay for, then a disciplined way to test the next step.

The work usually includes:

  • An offer a buyer can repeat, shared by the ad and the page
  • Account and campaign structure that matches that offer, not a catalog of audiences
  • Landing paths with one next step
  • Creative that says the same thing the site says
  • A rule for when spend may rise, tied to a conversion reading
  • A short operating rhythm: what gets reviewed, by whom, and what is not allowed to change on a hunch

This service sits inside Growth System and Interactive Flagship. It is a poor fit as a standalone media retainer when the page and the record are still broken. We will say so.

What we need from you

Access to the ad accounts, the current pages, and a person who can approve the offer. A rough sense of which customers you want more of, and which inquiries you are tired of receiving. You do not need a perfect pixel. You do need permission to pause what is leaking.

Connections

Ads depend on the website. The website depends on the CRM once someone raises a hand. Analytics has to be allowed to change the spend, or the reading is a souvenir.

If measurement is missing, the first build is the definition of a conversion, not a larger budget. That work lives with Conversion & Analytics and comes before a scale-up.

What good looks like

The ad, the page, and the first reply describe the same offer. A lead has an owner. The team can point at one reading and say whether the spend should hold, stop, or grow. Experiments are sequential enough to learn from.

No return is promised on this page. A live account gets a number only after you approve it.

Where this sits

  • Growth System, $25k-$45k. Not just a better site. A connected acquisition system.
  • Interactive Flagship, $45k-$85k+. Build the site people remember, and the system your team can actually run.

The rest of the system

  • Web Design & Development. The public instrument.
  • AI & Automation. The handoff, with an owner.
  • Conversion & Analytics. The reading.
