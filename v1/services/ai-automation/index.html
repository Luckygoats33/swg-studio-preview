The handoff, with an owner

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. AI & Automation

If the team is the integration, the system is not finished.

Routing, ownership, and follow-up so the founder is not the middleware. Automation removes copying. It does not hide the handoff.

  • Handoffs live in an inbox
  • People retype the same lead into another tool
  • Follow-up depends on memory

Most “AI projects” arrive as a request for a chatbot. The more expensive problem is usually older. A person copies an inquiry from a form into a spreadsheet, a CRM, and a message to sales. That person is the integration. When they are busy, the path stops.

SWG automates the copy and the route. We do not automate the judgment the business still has to make.

The break

The form works. The inbox receives it. After that, the path is folklore. Who replies, what they need to know, when delivery takes over, and what “done” means are all in people’s heads. Adding a model on top of that folklore makes a faster leak.

You can see it in the founder’s sent mail. The same context, written again, for a team that was already paid to know it.

The work

The work usually includes:

  • A map of the inquiry from submit to owner to next step
  • The record that map writes to, new or existing
  • Routing rules a human can read, not only a workflow canvas
  • Follow-up that fires when a step stalls, and a person who still owns the exception
  • Where a model helps: drafting a first reply, classifying a request, filling fields that are already known
  • Where a model stops: pricing, promises, and anything your counsel has not approved

We would rather connect a form to a CRM than demo an assistant that cannot see the customer.

What we need from you

The real path, including the ugly parts. Who actually replies today. Which tools are allowed to hold customer data. A decision on what the business will not automate. Access, and a person who feels the pain when a lead sits.

Connections

This service is the bridge from Website to CRM on the studio map, and from CRM toward a reading. Without Conversion & Analytics, you will not know whether the route is faster or only louder. Without a clear offer from Web Design, the automation will faithfully deliver a confused request.

What good looks like

A request creates a record without a forward. The record has an owner and a next step. Stalls are visible. The founder can leave for a week without becoming the backlog. The rules are short enough to argue with.

We will not publish hours saved for a template. A real engagement can, after you approve the count and the method.
