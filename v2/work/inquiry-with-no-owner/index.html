The inquiry with no owner

A template for documenting an inquiry path. This is not a client engagement and it reports no results.

  • Clarified
  • Converted
Before, intervention, after

Five steps, one inbox

A request reaches the site, lands in a personal inbox, and waits for the founder to forward it. Sales and delivery do not share a record.

Name the owner before the rebuild

The offer, the form, the owner, and the review rule are written down first. Tools come after the handoff is clear.

A request with a next step

The template ends with a path that has an owner and a record. It does not end with a metric. Numbers wait for client approval.

This entry shows the fields a real SWG Studio case study would carry. It is not a client. It is not a result.

The situation the template is shaped around is common. The business is paying for attention, or at least receiving it. The website asks for a conversation. The conversation arrives in one inbox. From there the path depends on memory.

Story mode, which you are reading, keeps the sequence in prose. Proof mode lists the objective, the intervention, the deliverables, and the empty slots where approved evidence would go. The scrubber above moves from the break, through the intervention, to the state after the path has an owner.

When a real study replaces this file, delete the example flag only after the client has approved every specific claim. Leave outcome notes empty of numbers until that approval exists.

Objective
Give every inquiry an owner and a next step the rest of the team can see.
Problem
Requests arrive through a form and then sit in one person's inbox. Sales waits for a forward. Delivery asks for the same context again. Nobody can say which inquiries were answered.
Intervention
Name the offer on the page, connect the form to a shared record, and write the handoff before any new campaign spend.
Deliverables
  • Message hierarchy for the public page
  • A single next step, with the owner named
  • Form to record routing
  • Handoff note for the people who respond
  • Event list for a later measurement pass
Stack

Illustrative for this template, not a client's production list.

  • Marketing site
  • Form handler
  • Shared inbox or CRM
  • Analytics events
Interaction notes
The public explanation is a three-stop scrubber (Before, Intervention, After) plus a Story and Proof toggle. Both work from native controls. Motion is not required to read the path.
Accessibility
No audit score is published. The studio standard for a real engagement is WCAG 2.2 AA, a keyboard path for every action, visible focus, and a static reading of any motion.
Performance
No performance score is published. A real engagement would report measured LCP, INP, and CLS after launch, with client approval. This template is text first and does not ship a hero image.
Outcomes
No results are published. Replace this note only with figures the client has approved. Do not invent a conversion rate, a revenue number, or a time saved.
Testimonial
None. Do not add a quote until the client has approved the words and the attribution.
